POLICY / 2026-08-28

Privacy, without fine print.

Your PDFs stay on your device

The desktop app reads, cleans, and hashes PDF files locally. It does not upload document contents, extracted text, filenames, hashes, or reports.

Files remain only where you choose to save them.

License data

The app stores your license token and its dated result after you activate Pro.

Verification sends that token to the Sociobot billing API. It never sends your PDF.

Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout as the merchant of record.

Website data

The site has no advertising trackers, analytics tools, outside fonts, or behavior cookies.

The download page requests public release details from the GitHub API. It caches successful details for one hour.

Hosting logs may contain an IP address, browser name, and requested path for security.

Your control

Delete the app’s local data to remove the cached license.

Delete clean PDFs and JSON reports from the places where you saved them.

Questions: privacy@sociobot.in.
